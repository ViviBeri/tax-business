FSSAI Licence / Registration Type Finder | KVB ENTERPRISES

A practical FSSAI category guide using the revised turnover thresholds effective from 1 April 2026.

From 1 April 2026, FSSAI revised the general turnover thresholds to Registration up to ₹1.5 crore, State Licence above ₹1.5 crore up to ₹50 crore, and Central Licence above ₹50 crore. Business activity, location and other criteria can affect the applicable category.
