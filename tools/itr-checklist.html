ITR Document Checklist

Use this as a general preparation guide. Exact requirements can vary by case.

  • PAN and Aadhaar details
  • Bank account information
  • Form 16 / salary details if applicable
  • Business/professional income records if applicable
  • Interest and other income details
  • Investment/deduction documents where applicable
  • Previous return / tax information if relevant
