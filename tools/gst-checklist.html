GST Registration Checklist

Use this as a general preparation guide. Exact requirements can vary by case.

  • PAN of proprietor/directors/partners as applicable
  • Aadhaar/identity and contact details
  • Business address proof
  • Bank account details/cancelled cheque where applicable
  • Photograph and business constitution documents as applicable
  • Digital signature where required
