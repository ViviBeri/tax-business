Puducherry GST registration assistance

We assist with the documentation and online application process for businesses that need GST registration. For a business operating in Puducherry, the GST state code is 34. GST applicability depends on the nature of the business, supplies and other facts of the case, so we review your situation before recommending a filing route.

Common documents and information

  • PAN and Aadhaar/identity details of the proprietor or authorised person
  • Business address and proof of principal place of business
  • Bank account details where applicable
  • Business constitution details, where applicable
  • Additional documents depending on the business and premises

The exact documents can vary. We confirm the list before you begin.

Puducherry GST FAQs

Is GST registration compulsory for every small business?

No. GST registration depends on applicable law and the business's circumstances. We can review your turnover, activity and supply pattern before you proceed.

Can you help with GST registration for a new shop?

Yes. We can help you understand the registration requirements, documents and application process for a new business.

Do I need a rented-shop agreement?

Premises documents depend on the nature of the premises and the case. We will tell you what is required after understanding your business and address.
