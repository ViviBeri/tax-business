Puducherry FSSAI FAQs

Does a home bakery need FSSAI?

Food businesses can be subject to FSSAI registration/licensing requirements. The appropriate category depends on the activity and applicable criteria. We can review your home bakery setup before you apply.

Can you help with FSSAI renewal?

Yes. We can assist with renewal and, where applicable, modification of an existing FSSAI registration/licence.

Do restaurants and cafés need FSSAI?

Food businesses generally need the applicable FSSAI registration or licence. The correct category depends on the business details.

What about a small tea or snack shop?

FoSCoS specifically includes petty retailers such as snack/tea shops among food-business categories. We can check the applicable route for your business.
