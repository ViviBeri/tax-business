GST in Karaikal: what to know

Karaikal is in the Union Territory of Puducherry. A business whose principal place of business is here registers for GST under Puducherry, so its GSTIN begins with 34. Karaikal is a region of the Union Territory of Puducherry, although it lies apart from Puducherry town, so registrations here are made under Puducherry rather than Tamil Nadu.

Which GST state code applies to a business in Karaikal?

Businesses in Karaikal register under Puducherry, and the first two digits of the GSTIN are the state or Union Territory code (34). The registration state follows your principal place of business, so we confirm it when we review your details.

Do I need GST registration for a small business in Karaikal?

It depends on factors such as turnover, the kind of goods or services you supply, and whether you sell across states or online. We review your case before recommending registration. The GST Registration Eligibility Guide gives an indicative first check.
