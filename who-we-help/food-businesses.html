Common food-business situations

Restaurant / café

FSSAI and tax registrations may be relevant, with GST depending on the circumstances.

Home bakery

FSSAI is separate from GST. The right requirements depend on your activity, turnover and supply model.

Cloud kitchen

Food licensing, tax compliance and business documentation should be reviewed together.

Current FSSAI note: From 1 April 2026, the general turnover bands are Registration up to ₹1.5 crore, State Licence above ₹1.5 crore to ₹50 crore, and Central Licence above ₹50 crore. Activity-specific rules can differ.
