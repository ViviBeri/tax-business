Common questions

Do all freelancers need GST?

Not necessarily. Applicability depends on turnover, supply type and compulsory-registration situations.

Can I get Udyam?

Eligible enterprises can use the official Udyam portal. KVB provides paid professional assistance.

What about ITR?

Income-tax filing depends on your income, status and applicable filing rules.
