FSSAI Registration & Food Licence Assistance

FSSAI registration or licence is part of the food-safety licensing framework for covered Food Business Operators (FBOs). KVB ENTERPRISES helps local food businesses understand the applicable category, prepare the relevant information and documents, and submit the application through the official FoSCoS system.

2026 update: From 1 April 2026, FSSAI revised the turnover thresholds and introduced perpetual validity for registrations and licences, subject to ongoing compliance and the applicable rules.

Who may need FSSAI?

Common covered food-business activities include:

🍰 Food & HomeHome bakers, tiffin services, small food businesses and petty food operators.
🍽️ Food ServiceRestaurants, cafés, cloud kitchens, caterers, canteens and similar businesses.
🏪 Retail & TradeFood retailers, wholesalers, distributors, grocery businesses and direct sellers.
🏭 ManufacturingFood manufacturers, processors, repackers and other applicable food businesses.
🚚 LogisticsEligible storage and transport businesses dealing with food.
🌐 Import / ExportFood importers, exporters and eligible e-commerce food businesses.
Important: The correct category depends on the Kind of Business (KoB), activity, turnover and other applicable criteria. Turnover alone does not determine every FSSAI category.

Which FSSAI category may apply?

CategoryCurrent turnover thresholdImportant note
RegistrationUp to ₹1.5 croreFor eligible food businesses covered by the registration criteria.

FSSAI FAQs

Do home bakers need FSSAI?

A home-based food business may fall under the FSSAI registration/licensing framework. The applicable category depends on the activity and current criteria.

What are the current FSSAI turnover thresholds?

From 1 April 2026, the revised thresholds are Registration up to ₹1.5 crore, State Licence above ₹1.5 crore to ₹50 crore, and Central Licence above ₹50 crore, subject to the applicable KoB and other criteria.

Does FSSAI need renewal every year now?

FSSAI registrations and licences issued under the revised framework have perpetual validity unless suspended, cancelled or surrendered. Applicable food-safety and other compliance requirements continue.

Can a street-food vendor have a special rule?

FSSAI has introduced deemed-registration provisions for certain street-food vendors already registered under the Street Vendors Act, subject to the stated conditions and hygiene requirements.
