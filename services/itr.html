Income Tax / ITR Filing Assistance

KVB ENTERPRISES assists individuals, proprietors and eligible businesses with income-tax return preparation and filing. We help organise income, tax and supporting information, select the appropriate return route and coordinate with a Chartered Accountant where professional certification or tax-audit work is required.

Important: The correct ITR form and filing requirements depend on the taxpayer, sources of income, turnover, audit applicability and other facts. We do not treat every taxpayer as the same.

Who may need ITR assistance?

IndividualsSalary, pension, interest, capital gains, property income and other applicable income.
ProprietorsBusiness or professional income requiring an appropriate business return.
FreelancersProfessional receipts, expenses, TDS and related income information.
Partners / FirmsReturn assistance and coordination for applicable firm/partner situations.
BusinessesCompanies and other entities may have separate return and compliance requirements.
Tax noticesInitial document review and coordination support; representation by a qualified professional where required.

Documents & information required

Your checklist depends on your income sources and taxpayer type. Common items include:

IncomeSalary/Form 16, business/professional income records, rent, interest and other income details.
Tax statementsAIS/TIS, Form 26AS, TDS/TCS details and tax-payment information as applicable.
Bank & investmentsBank statements and relevant investment/capital-gains information where applicable.
PropertyHome-loan interest, rent/property income and sale/purchase information where applicable.
BusinessBooks/summary, turnover, expenses, GST information and other records depending on the case.
IdentityPAN, Aadhaar/contact details and prior return information where relevant.

Common ITR forms

FormTypical useImportant note
ITR-1Eligible resident individuals with specified simple income profiles within the applicable limits.Not suitable for every individual taxpayer.
ITR-2Individuals/HUFs without business or professional income, including certain capital-gain/property cases.Eligibility depends on the detailed conditions for the year.
ITR-3Individuals/HUFs having business or professional income.Books, business details and audit applicability may matter.
ITR-4Eligible taxpayers using the presumptive taxation framework subject to its conditions.Not available for every business/professional situation.

The Income Tax Department publishes the current ITR forms and instructions for each assessment year. Official ITR downloads.

ITR FAQs

Can KVB file my ITR?

Yes, for applicable cases we provide ITR preparation and filing assistance based on the information you provide.

Do you handle business ITRs?

Yes, we assist eligible proprietors and businesses and identify when additional professional review is needed.

What if my case requires a tax audit?

Tax audit work is carried out through a Chartered Accountant where required. KVB can coordinate the documents and filing workflow.

What documents do I need?

It depends on your income sources. We provide a case-specific checklist instead of asking every taxpayer for the same documents.

Can you help reconcile AIS/26AS?

We can help organise and review the information and identify mismatches that need clarification.

Do you guarantee a refund?

No. Refunds depend on the taxpayer’s actual income, taxes paid, deductions and the return processed by the Income Tax Department.

What is your ITR fee?

Non-audit ITR pricing depends on the taxpayer and complexity. ITR with tax-audit support is ₹5,000 onwards, with CA coordination where required.

Can you handle tax notices?

We can provide initial document review and coordination. Formal representation or professional opinions may require a CA or tax professional depending on the matter.
