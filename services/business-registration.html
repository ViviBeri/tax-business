Business Registration & Setup Assistance

Choosing the right business structure affects documentation, ownership, taxation, filings and ongoing compliance. KVB ENTERPRISES helps new and existing business owners understand the registration route and coordinates the applicable documentation and filing process.

Important: “Business registration” can mean different things depending on the structure and activity. A proprietorship, partnership, LLP, company and local establishment registration are not the same legal process.

Business structures we can help you understand

ProprietorshipSuitable for many owner-operated small businesses, subject to the applicable registrations and local requirements.
PartnershipFor businesses owned by two or more partners under a partnership arrangement.
LLPA separate legal structure with LLP-specific incorporation and ongoing compliance.
Private Limited / OPCCompany structures registered through the MCA process with company-law compliance.
Local registrationsShop/establishment or municipal registrations may apply separately depending on location and activity.
Food / regulated businessesAdditional registrations such as FSSAI may apply based on the activity.

Documents & information required

The exact checklist depends on the chosen structure.

StructureCommon information/documents
ProprietorshipOwner PAN/Aadhaar, business address, business activity, bank/contact information and applicable supporting documents.
PartnershipPartner identity details, partnership deed, business address and applicable registrations.
LLPPartner/designated partner details, identity/address proofs, proposed name, registered office details, DSC and LLP incorporation documents as applicable.
CompanyDirector/subscriber identity and address details, registered office proof/NOC, proposed name and incorporation documents including MOA/AOA as applicable.
Local establishmentOwner/entity details, premises proof, rent/NOC/property documents and other items prescribed by the relevant local authority.

For company/LLP incorporation, professional certification or drafting requirements may apply. We will tell you when a CA/CS/lawyer or other authorised professional needs to be involved.

Business Registration FAQs

Which structure is best for my business?

There is no single best structure for everyone. It depends on ownership, liability, funding, compliance expectations, tax considerations and business plans.

Can you register a proprietorship?

We can assist with the applicable registrations and documentation used to establish and operate a proprietorship business.

Do you handle LLP and company incorporation?

We can coordinate the applicable process and documentation. Where statutory professional certification or drafting is required, the work will be carried out through the appropriate authorised professional.

Do I need GST immediately after starting a business?

Not every new business has the same GST requirement. Applicability depends on the business activity, turnover, supply pattern and other rules.

Do I need Udyam as well?

Udyam is a separate MSME registration. It may be useful for eligible enterprises but is not the same as business incorporation or GST registration.

Can a home business be registered?

Many home-based businesses can use appropriate business/registration routes, but the requirements depend on the activity and local rules.

What does KVB charge?

Business-registration pricing depends on the structure and scope. Contact us for a case-specific quote; statutory/government charges are separate where applicable.

Do you guarantee approval?

No. Approval is subject to the relevant authority, documents, professional certification requirements and applicable law.
