1. Overview

Udyam Registration is the Government of India's MSME registration system. An eligible enterprise receives a permanent Udyam Registration Number and an online Udyam Registration Certificate. The official portal states that there is no renewal requirement for the registration.

Udyam is separate from GST registration, FSSAI registration, trade licences and other business registrations. Whether you need any other registration depends on your business, activity, turnover, location and applicable rules.

Official source: Udyam Registration Portal

3. Who can register?

Any person intending to establish an eligible micro, small or medium enterprise may apply through the Udyam system, subject to the applicable rules and classification criteria.

Retail & Trading

Eligible retail and wholesale trade activities covered by the applicable Udyam/NIC framework may be registered.

Service Businesses

Consultants, professionals, agencies and other eligible service enterprises can consider Udyam registration.

Manufacturing

Eligible manufacturers and production units can register and specify their applicable activities.

Food Businesses

Bakeries, food businesses and other eligible enterprises may consider Udyam alongside any food-specific registrations that apply.

Home & Online Businesses

Home-based and online enterprises can explore Udyam eligibility based on their actual business activity and structure.

New Enterprises

A person intending to establish a micro, small or medium enterprise may apply through the official portal.

Important: Udyam registration does not replace GST, FSSAI, Shops & Establishments or other registrations that may apply to a particular business.

4. Benefits & practical uses

Udyam registration can provide formal MSME recognition and may support access to applicable government schemes, procurement opportunities, lending-related benefits and other MSME support measures, subject to each scheme's eligibility rules.

  • Formal recognition as an MSME/Udyam enterprise
  • Eligibility consideration for applicable MSME schemes
  • Potential access to applicable procurement/support programmes
  • Use of the Udyam certificate where an MSME registration is requested
  • Possible relevance to priority-sector lending and other notified measures
  • One Udyam Registration can include multiple manufacturing/service activities
No automatic benefit: Udyam registration by itself does not guarantee a loan, subsidy, tender, tax benefit or scheme approval. Each programme has its own conditions.

6. Information required

Udyam is paperless and the official portal states that documents/proof normally do not need to be uploaded for registration. However, you still need accurate business information for the application.

Identity

  • Aadhaar number of the appropriate person
  • Name as per Aadhaar
  • PAN

Business details

  • Enterprise/business name
  • Organisation type
  • Date of commencement
  • Main activity and NIC activity

Address & contact

  • Official business address
  • Plant/unit details where applicable
  • Mobile/email and other contact information

Financial information

  • Investment details
  • Turnover details
  • GST-linked information where applicable

Constitution-specific

  • For proprietorship: proprietor's Aadhaar
  • For partnership: managing partner's Aadhaar
  • For HUF: Karta's Aadhaar

Company / LLP / other bodies

  • Authorised person's Aadhaar
  • PAN
  • GSTIN where applicable
  • Organisation details
Documents vs. information: The official portal says Udyam registration is paperless and no documents or proof are required to be uploaded. We may still ask you for supporting information so we can check the details before submitting the application.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

Is Udyam Registration free?

Yes. The official Udyam portal states that there is no fee for filing Udyam Registration. KVB's ₹1,000 is an optional professional assistance fee if you want help with the process.

Is Udyam registration the same as GST registration?

No. Udyam/MSME registration and GST registration are separate registrations with different purposes. A business may need one, both or other registrations depending on its circumstances.

Do I need to upload Aadhaar, PAN or other documents?

The official portal states that Udyam registration is paperless and that documents/proof do not need to be uploaded. Aadhaar is required for the registration process, and applicable PAN/GST information is used as specified by the official system.

Can a small shop or trader register?

Eligible retail and wholesale trade activities are included in the Udyam framework subject to the applicable NIC/activity rules and conditions.

Can a service business or freelancer register?

Eligible service enterprises can register subject to the applicable MSME classification and activity rules. We can review your activity before you apply.

Can a new business register?

Yes, the official portal allows a person intending to establish a micro, small or medium enterprise to file for Udyam Registration, subject to the applicable requirements.

Does Udyam Registration expire?

The official Udyam portal states that there is no need for renewal of the registration.

Does Udyam registration guarantee a government loan or subsidy?

No. Udyam registration does not guarantee a loan, subsidy, tender or other benefit. Each scheme or lender has separate eligibility and approval conditions.

Can I add more than one business activity?

The official portal states that an enterprise should not file more than one Udyam Registration, but multiple activities, including manufacturing or services or both, may be specified or added in one registration.

Can KVB help update an existing Udyam certificate?

Yes, we can assist with eligible update/correction requirements. The exact process depends on what needs to be changed.
