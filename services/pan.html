Safe, Secure & Hassle-Free PAN Assistance

KVB ENTERPRISES provides assistance with PAN-related applications and corrections for individuals and eligible applicants. We help you understand the appropriate request, organise the information and guide you through the application process.

Important: PAN is issued by the Income Tax Department through the prescribed application process. KVB ENTERPRISES is an independent assistance service and is not a government PAN office.

What PAN assistance can cover

New PANAssistance with a new PAN application for eligible applicants.
CorrectionName, date of birth and other eligible detail corrections.
Address / detailsGuidance for updating eligible PAN details through the prescribed process.
Lost / damaged PANAssistance with reprint/reissue-related requests and available alternatives.
PAN-AadhaarGeneral assistance for checking or resolving applicable PAN-Aadhaar linking issues.
Business PANGuidance for entities and organisations requiring PAN-related application support.

Who may need PAN assistance?

Individuals, proprietors, firms, companies, trusts and other entities may have PAN-related requirements depending on their status and transactions.

Documents & information

Individual applicantsIdentity, address and date-of-birth information/proof as applicable to the selected application route.
Entity applicantsConstitution/entity documents and authorised-person details as applicable.
CorrectionExisting PAN details plus supporting proof for the field being corrected, where required.
Contact detailsMobile/email information for communication or verification where applicable.

The exact proof list depends on the applicant type and the requested change. We confirm the applicable checklist before filing.

PAN FAQs

Can I apply for PAN through KVB?

Yes. We provide application assistance and document guidance for eligible PAN requests.

Can you correct my PAN name or date of birth?

Yes, where the requested correction is supported by the prescribed proof.

I lost my PAN card. Do I need a new PAN?

Usually the requirement is to obtain a reprint/reissue of the existing PAN rather than applying for a second PAN. We can help identify the appropriate route.

Can you help with PAN-Aadhaar issues?

We can provide general assistance and help identify the next step based on the status shown by the official system.

Is PAN application approval guaranteed?

No. PAN issuance/correction is subject to the prescribed verification and authority process.

How much does PAN assistance cost?

Contact KVB for current pricing based on the type of PAN service required.

Is my PAN information safe?

We follow a document-minimisation approach and only request information needed for the service. Do not send unnecessary sensitive documents through public channels.
