What is GST Return Filing?

GST return filing is the periodic reporting of applicable sales/outward supplies, input tax credit and tax liability through the GST system. The returns and filing frequency depend on the taxpayer's registration, scheme, turnover and applicable rules.

Important: Filing requirements are profile-specific. We check your GST registration and filing pattern before taking up recurring work.

Common GST filings

GSTR-1

Statement of outward supplies, filed monthly or quarterly depending on the taxpayer and applicable filing scheme.

GSTR-3B

Summary return reporting applicable outward supplies, input tax credit and tax liability/payment.

GSTR-9

Annual return, where applicable, subject to the taxpayer's eligibility and exemption rules for the relevant financial year.

GSTR-2B

Auto-generated input tax credit statement. It is not a return to be filed, but is useful for ITC reconciliation.

What we need from you each filing period

  • Sales invoices / sales summary
  • Purchase invoices and expense documents relevant to GST
  • Credit/debit notes, if any
  • Details of exports, imports, reverse charge or other special transactions, if applicable
  • Bank/payment information where needed for reconciliation
  • Previous return/ledger information when starting with an existing GSTIN

The exact data checklist depends on your business and filing profile.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which GST returns will you file?

The applicable return depends on your GST profile and filing scheme. Common filings include GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B; other returns may apply in specific cases.

Do I need to send purchase invoices?

Usually, purchase and expense information relevant to GST and ITC is important. We will give you the period-specific checklist.

Do you check ITC?

Where included in the agreed scope, we can assist with GST data and ITC reconciliation. ITC eligibility remains subject to the GST law and the taxpayer's records.

What if I have no sales in a month?

A registered taxpayer may still have a filing obligation even when there are no outward taxable sales. We will check the applicable return and filing requirement for your GST profile.

Can you handle old pending GST returns?
