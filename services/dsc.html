Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) Assistance

A Digital Signature Certificate is issued by a Certifying Authority licensed under India's PKI framework. KVB ENTERPRISES helps customers identify the appropriate DSC requirement, organise the applicant information and coordinate the issuance process with a licensed Certifying Authority.

Important: The Controller of Certifying Authorities (CCA) licenses and regulates Certifying Authorities; the CCA itself does not issue end-entity DSCs. DSCs are issued by licensed Certifying Authorities.

DSC basics

Individual DSCFor an individual subscriber where a digital signature is required by an application or portal.
Organisation useDirectors, authorised persons and other subscribers may require DSCs for specific filings or systems.
Assurance levelThe appropriate certificate depends on the portal/application and current Certifying Authority requirements.

Official CCA guidance says applicants should approach a licensed Certifying Authority for a DSC. Licensed CA services.

Common uses

  • MCA/company and LLP filings where DSC is required
  • GST and other government/e-governance filings where a DSC is prescribed
  • Income-tax/e-filing or other portals where the applicable signing method requires DSC
  • E-procurement and other digital-signature-enabled systems

The exact DSC type and signing requirement depends on the portal, role and current rules.

Documents / information required

IdentityPAN/Aadhaar or other identity information required by the selected licensed CA and verification method.
AddressAddress information/proof as required by the Certifying Authority's verification process.
ContactMobile/email details for verification and certificate communication.
Role / organisationCompany/LLP/entity details and role information where the DSC is for an organisational filing.

DSC FAQs

Does CCA issue my DSC?

No. CCA licenses and regulates Certifying Authorities. A licensed CA issues the DSC to the subscriber.

Do I need a DSC for every government portal?

No. The requirement depends on the particular portal, filing and role.

Can you arrange a DSC?

We can assist with the application and coordinate with a licensed Certifying Authority.

What documents are needed?

Identity, address, contact and role/entity information may be required depending on the CA and verification method.

How much does DSC cost?

Contact KVB for current pricing because the certificate type, validity, token and issuing CA can affect the total cost.

Is a DSC legally recognised?

Digital signatures issued within the Indian PKI framework have legal recognition under the Information Technology Act framework, subject to the applicable requirements.
